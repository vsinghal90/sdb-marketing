# Bollywood parody reel: "Mere paas garam tawa hai" (draft, 30s, 9:16)

A spoof of the "Mere paas maa hai" scene. Two parathas (owner change, 2026-10-10). One labelled "Cooked from frozen" (cut/frozenparatha.webp: pale, flat, frosty, wears sunglasses) shows off its freezer, microwave and six-month shelf life, then asks "Tumhare paas kya hai?". After a dramatic pause the golden one labelled "Ghar jaisa" answers "Mere paas... garam tawa hai." The frozen paratha melts, the paratha puts on sunglasses, and the offer and end card follow.
Artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/FNvpGt7h7THrxZUaUC6JDe (built 2026-10-10). No MP4 yet; render one only when the owner says go.

Timeline (23.6s, scene lengths in the S array; the offer scene with the meal photo and prices was dropped by the owner on 2026-10-10): hook 2s (both parathas from frame one, zoom and shake, "Wait for the reply") · show-off 7.2s (one line per shot, 1s cuts to the ghar jaisa paratha looking intimidated, with gulps) · question 1.8s (extreme close-up, shake) · intimidated close-up 2s · punchline 3.6s (flash and shake on "garam tawa hai") · melt 3s · end card with the 12A sound logo 4s. The music in score() follows the scene start times, so changing a scene length moves its cues too.
Editing rules the owner asked for: no captions describing the music or sound; a visual hook in the first second; a cut every 1 to 1.5 seconds; S[i].shots and S[i].fx in the template drive the cuts and the shake/flash.
Safe zone: all text and characters sit inside .box, which is exactly the safe zone (top 14%, bottom 28%, 12% sides). #safe shows the guide.
Music: an original synthesised 70s-filmi parody score (score() in the template), no film audio. Voices: Hindi dialogue clips in voice/*.wav (f1-f4 frozen paratha, pitched down in the page; p1-p2 ghar jaisa paratha), made offline with the Kokoro TTS model (voice/make_voices.py; the model files are not kept here). The music ducks under them; V mutes them. Captions stay on screen for muted viewers.
Gaze: captions sit at about 23-45% of the frame height and faces at 45-60%, the centre band viewers watch on short videos.
Characters: cut/paratha.webp is a circular crop of the torn paneer paratha from cut/paneer.webp; faces are SVG overlays. cut/frozenparatha.webp is the butter paratha from the same photo, made pale and frosty in PIL.
Build: python3 build.py (writes sdb-parody-reel.html). Frame check: in the browser console, seek(16.5).
Costumes (owner asked for parathas resembling the two brothers, then "massy", 2026-10-10): the frozen paratha is the rich elder brother (towering 70s puffed hair, long sideburns, thick angry brows over sunglasses, dark suit with a popped wide white collar); the ghar jaisa paratha is the filmi police inspector (khaki peaked cap with badge, thick handlebar moustache, khaki uniform, lathi on the shoulder). Labels sit on their chests like name plates. Hook: triple zoom on "dhan dhan dhaaan"; punchline: rotating hero light rays. Costume and hair only, never an actor's face, name or voice.
Full figures (owner, 2026-10-10): both are full-body now. Elder brother: tall and lean (head 21cqw), black shirt under a grey suit, grey flared trousers, hands in pockets, black shoes (the scene's black shirt and grey suit come from a review of the film). Close-ups scale the figure so the head is about 50cqw and the legs leave the frame.
Background (owner, 2026-10-10): each video gets its own background. This one is the film setting, a night street under a bridge with a street lamp, a parked 70s car and the city through the arch (.setbg SVG). The end card fades back to the brand green.
